IRUL YouTube Uploader

IRUL YouTube Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the IRUL (I Read U Learn) YouTube channel. It is not offered to anyone else.

It uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload the channel's own short videos and schedule when they go public;
  • check whether a scheduled video has been published;
  • edit the titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists of the channel's own videos;
  • read, reply to and moderate comments on the channel's own videos;
  • read the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports (totals such as views and watch time).

It never accesses any other channel's private data and never shows YouTube data to other people.
