Privacy Policy

IRUL Uploader · effective 20 November 2026

Who uses this app

IRUL Uploader is used only by the owner of the IRUL (I Read U Learn) YouTube channel, to manage that channel. It has no other users.

YouTube API Services

This app uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • The channel's own videos and their details (title, description, privacy status, publish time).
  • Comments on the channel's own videos, including the public channel name of each commenter, only to read, reply to or moderate them.
  • The channel's own YouTube Analytics reports. These are totals (for example views, watch time, age groups, countries, traffic sources). YouTube does not give the app any data that identifies individual viewers, and the app does not try to identify them.

How the data is used

Only to upload and schedule the channel's videos, check that they were published, manage their details and comments, and review the channel's own performance.

Storage, sharing and deletion

  • The sign-in tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer and in the owner's private, encrypted storage. They are never published.
  • Video IDs and publish status are kept in the owner's private notes. Analytics figures kept there are refreshed or deleted within 30 days.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No cookies or tracking are used by this app or this website.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After access is revoked, stored data from the YouTube API Services is deleted within 30 days.
